个人简介About §

秦慧玲，博士，北京师范大学人工智能与未来网络研究院助理教授、博士生导师。自 2017 年起师从城市计算领域奠基人、IEEE Fellow 郑宇教授，长期专注于时空数据挖掘与城市计算领域的研究工作，围绕时空表征学习这一核心方向在 ICML、KDD、AAAI、TKDE 等国际顶级学术会议和期刊发表多篇论文，拥有 2 项国家发明专利。主持国家自然科学基金青年项目 1 项，参与国家重点研发计划等多项国家级项目。先后在微软亚洲研究院、滴滴大数据研究院和京东科技等企业担任算法研究员，利用智能算法在信号灯优化、危化品违规行为识别和地址解析等业务场景中解决了关键问题。获得陕西高校优秀毕业生、西安电子科技大学优秀博士论文等荣誉。

每年招收硕士 2 名，博士 1 名，有意向者请发送简历到联系邮箱。

Dr. Qin Huiling is an Assistant Professor and Doctoral Supervisor at the Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Future Networks, Beijing Normal University. Since 2017, she has been studying under Professor Yu Zheng, a pioneer in urban computing and IEEE Fellow, dedicating herself to research in spatiotemporal data mining and urban computing. Focusing on spatiotemporal representation learning as her core direction, she has published numerous papers in top-tier venues including ICML, KDD, AAAI, and TKDE, and holds two national invention patents. She serves as the Principal Investigator for one National Natural Science Foundation Youth Project and has participated in multiple national-level projects. She previously held positions as Algorithm Researcher at Microsoft Research Asia, DiDi Big Data Research, and JD Technology, applying intelligent algorithms to challenges in traffic signal optimization, hazardous-chemical violation identification, and address parsing. She has received honors including Outstanding Graduate of Shaanxi Universities and Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation Award from Xidian University.
