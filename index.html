About Me

Dr. Qin Huiling is an Assistant Professor and Doctoral Supervisor at the Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Future Networks, Beijing Normal University. Since 2017, she has been studying under Professor Yu Zheng, a pioneer in the field of urban computing and IEEE Fellow, dedicating herself to research in spatiotemporal data mining and urban computing. Focusing on spatiotemporal representation learning as her core research direction, she has published numerous papers in top-tier international academic conferences and journals including ICML, KDD, AAAI, and TKDE, and holds two national invention patents. She serves as the Principal Investigator for one National Natural Science Foundation of China Youth Project and has participated in multiple national-level projects including the National Key Research and Development Program. She has held positions as Algorithm Researcher at Microsoft Research Asia, DiDi Big Data Research Institute, and JD Technology, where she applied intelligent algorithms to address critical challenges in various business scenarios such as traffic signal optimization, identification of hazardous chemical violations, and address parsing. She has received honors including Outstanding Graduate of Shaanxi Universities and Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation Award from Xidian University.
